About Us

Phone Screen Test is a free web tool with one job: help you check whether your screen is healthy — dead pixels, stuck pixels, uneven colors, backlight bleed, touch dead zones and refresh-rate problems, all in one place.

That need is real. A new phone can arrive with a dead pixel you only notice weeks later, and a used phone can hide a faint burn-in mark or a touch dead zone that only shows up in one corner. Finding these problems by eye is hard without the right patterns on screen — a plain white wallpaper will not show a stuck red pixel, and a photo will not show backlight bleed. This tool puts the right pattern on your screen, one test at a time.

That is why we built this tool for the browser. Open the page, tap Start Screen Test, and the checks run one after another in fullscreen — no app, no download, no account. It works on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops, because every one of them has a browser.

What the tool does

  • 57 display tests in 8 categories — Pixel & Display, Color, Brightness & Uniformity, Touch, Motion & Refresh, Image Quality, OLED & Panel Health, and Orientation.
  • Solid Color Test — one run through white, black, red, green, blue and gray, the classic way to catch dead or stuck pixels.
  • Touch tests — coverage, multi-touch, accuracy, edges, corners and dead zones, so you can find the exact spot that does not respond.
  • Brightness and backlight checks — bleed, clouding and uniformity, best checked in a dim room.
  • Motion and refresh rate — moving patterns and a browser-measured refresh-rate reading.
  • Results summary — mark each test "Looks good" or "Problem found" and get a summary at the end, with a button to re-run just the failed tests.

Who we are

We also run the SpeakerCleaners tool, the White Screen test and the Phone Screen Resolution tool. Phone Screen Test is our fourth free tool — same idea, different question: instead of cleaning a speaker or measuring a resolution, it checks whether the screen itself is healthy.

Our promise

The tool is free and will stay free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no locked features and no downloads. We do not collect your data — every test runs on your own device, in your browser, and nothing leaves it.

If the tool helped you check a new phone — or catch a problem before a return window closed — share it with someone shopping for their next screen.
