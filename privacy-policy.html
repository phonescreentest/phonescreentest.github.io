Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Phone Screen Test ("we", "us", "our") runs this website, a free phone screen test tool. This policy explains what information we collect — in short: we do not collect personal information from visitors.

Information we do not collect

Using the screen test does not require an account, a sign-up, or any personal details. The tests run on your own device, in your browser, and are shown only to you. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or sent to any server. We do not ask for your name, phone number, location, or device information to use the tool. Your test choices are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so the tool remembers which tests you picked — that data never leaves your device.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set tracking cookies and does not use analytics that identify individual visitors. Your browser may store the page in its own cache so it can load faster next time, but that is controlled by your browser, not by us.

Contact form

If you contact us through the contact page, your email app sends us the message you wrote, including the name and email address you provided. We use that information only to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone else or use it for marketing.

Third-party links

This site links to our SpeakerCleaners tool, our White Screen test and our Phone Screen Resolution tool. If you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply there. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

Children

This website is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how this site handles information, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. Because we collect no visitor data, any change would be minor.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit our contact page.
